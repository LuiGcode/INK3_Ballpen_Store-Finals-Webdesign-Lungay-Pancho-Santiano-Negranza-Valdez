We imagined a stationery store built around one simple thought: choosing a pen should be easy and enjoyable.

Meet INK3

Thoughtful tools for everyday stories.

INK3 is a fictional ballpen brand created as a school e-commerce project for FEU Diliman's BA0013L — Web Client-Side Scripting. The concept is to make pen shopping feel focused and uncomplicated.

Instead of a crowded catalog, the website offers only three types of ballpens: the dependable Everyday, the smooth-writing SmoothWrite, and the premium-inspired Signature.

Every element is designed to help visitors compare products, understand prices, and explore a demonstration shopping experience.

Good to know

Frequently
asked.

Are these actual ballpens for sale?

No. INK3 is a fictional concept brand built as an academic project. The prices, product descriptions, and illustrations are illustrative only.

Can I place an actual order?

No. The shopping cart and checkout are working client-side demonstrations. They do not connect to fulfillment or payment services.

How does the shopping bag work?

The cart uses your browser's local storage to remember product quantities on the same browser. You can add, remove, and change the quantity of any of the three pens.

Does the demo checkout store my address?

No. The checkout form only validates fields in your browser. It does not transmit or save names, emails, or addresses. For testing, please use invented details.

Is INK3 mobile-friendly?

Yes. The layout adapts to desktop, tablet, and phone screens, including a mobile menu and responsive product cards.
